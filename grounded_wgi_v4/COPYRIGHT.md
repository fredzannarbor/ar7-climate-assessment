## IPCC reports are NOT public domain

Genuine IPCC assessment reports are **copyrighted** works — published by
Cambridge University Press, © IPCC. They are not public domain, and nothing in
this project treats them as such.

This project therefore does **not** reproduce, excerpt, translate, or derive its
text from any IPCC report. What it imitates is the **format and register** of an
IPCC Working Group assessment — the calibrated-uncertainty language, the lettered
headline statements, the chapter architecture — none of which is itself protected
expression. The substantive content is synthesized from a separately harvested
corpus of **openly indexed, independently published, DOI-bearing journal articles**
(via OpenAlex), not from IPCC text.

"AR7" and "Working Group I" name the *target format the model was asked to imitate*.
This work is not affiliated with, endorsed by, reviewed by, or connected to the
IPCC, Cambridge University Press, the WMO, or UNEP.

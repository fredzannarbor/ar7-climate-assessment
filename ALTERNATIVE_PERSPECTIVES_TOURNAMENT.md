# Alternative Climate Adaptation Perspectives Tournament

## Overview

This tournament framework compares 16 radically different climate adaptation strategies, all accepting AR7 scientific assessment as factual baseline while proposing diverse implementation approaches.

**Key Principle**: All perspectives must accept the climate science as accurate. They differ in values, governance models, and strategic approaches - not in denial or acceptance of facts.

---

## The 16 Perspectives

### 1. **Amazon Six-Pager**
- **Worldview**: Corporate efficiency, mechanisms-driven, customer-obsessed
- **Key Features**: Flywheel effects, ownership accountability, measurable outcomes
- **Governance**: Private sector leadership with clear KPIs

### 2. **Indigenous-First Policy**
- **Worldview**: Traditional Ecological Knowledge, seven-generation thinking
- **Key Features**: FPIC, land sovereignty, Indigenous self-determination
- **Governance**: Nation-to-nation relationships, Indigenous-led institutions

### 3. **Chinese-Led Policy**
- **Worldview**: Ecological civilization, centralized planning, infrastructure
- **Key Features**: Sponge cities, Belt & Road greening, South-South cooperation
- **Governance**: Coordinated state capacity, Five-Year Plans

### 4. **Golden Age SF Triumphalism**
- **Worldview**: Human ingenuity, technological mastery, abundance
- **Key Features**: Fusion energy, mega-engineering, space infrastructure
- **Governance**: Manhattan Project-scale R&D, public-private moonshots

### 5. **Geoengineering Commitment**
- **Worldview**: Pragmatic intervention, risk-risk tradeoffs
- **Key Features**: SRM, CDR at scale, adaptive governance
- **Governance**: International treaty with mandatory participation

### 6. **Solarpunk Optimism**
- **Worldview**: Distributed systems, beautiful green tech, cooperation
- **Key Features**: Community energy, green infrastructure, appropriate tech
- **Governance**: Participatory democracy, cooperative economics

### 7. **Islamic Climate Justice**
- **Worldview**: Khilafah (stewardship), justice (adl), balance (mizan)
- **Key Features**: Zakah/waqf finance, OIC coordination, halal green economy
- **Governance**: Islamic principles in climate governance, faith-based mobilization

### 8. **SIDS Survival Imperative**
- **Worldview**: Existential justice, climate as survival threat
- **Key Features**: Loss & damage priority, ocean solutions, AOSIS leadership
- **Governance**: Small states' moral authority, international solidarity

### 9. **Degrowth/Post-Capitalist**
- **Worldview**: Growth as problem, sufficiency over efficiency
- **Key Features**: Care economy, commons governance, work reduction
- **Governance**: Economic democracy, post-capitalist institutions

### 10. **Bioregional Localism**
- **Worldview**: Watersheds as organizing units, place-based knowledge
- **Key Features**: Foodsheds, ecological restoration, reinhabitation
- **Governance**: Bioregional assemblies, nested local-to-global

### 11. **Antarctic Treaty Model**
- **Worldview**: Global commons, science diplomacy, depoliticization
- **Key Features**: Mandatory cooperation, transparency, environmental protocols
- **Governance**: Treaty-based with consensus decision-making

### 12. **Evil Billionaire Cabal** (Thought Experiment)
- **Worldview**: Elite wealth preservation, power maintenance
- **Key Features**: Private refuges, adaptation infrastructure control, strategic philanthropy
- **Governance**: Secret coordination, public-private capture

### 13. **Post-WWIII Reconstruction**
- **Worldview**: Catastrophe as opportunity, build back better
- **Key Features**: Climate-proof from start, no fossil lock-in, knowledge preservation
- **Governance**: New climate-centered constitutions, global cooperation

### 14. **Anarchist Mutual Aid**
- **Worldview**: Horizontal organization, voluntary cooperation, abolition of hierarchy
- **Key Features**: Neighborhood networks, autonomous zones, direct action
- **Governance**: Consensus-based federation, dual power strategy

### 15. **AI-Governed Technocracy**
- **Worldview**: Algorithmic optimization beyond human bias
- **Key Features**: Central Climate AI, real-time Earth monitoring, data-driven allocation
- **Governance**: AI decision-making with human value alignment

### 16. **Youth Climate Uprising**
- **Worldview**: Intergenerational justice, generational betrayal
- **Key Features**: Climate strikes, youth councils, system change demands
- **Governance**: Youth-led with elder allies, direct action + electoral

## Tournament Structure

### Phase 1: Generation (Weeks 1-4)

**Objective**: Generate all 16 perspective documents using multiple LLMs

#### Models to Test
- **Tier 1 (Premium)**: GPT-5, Claude Sonnet 4.5, Gemini 2.5 Pro, Grok 3
- **Tier 2 (Standard)**: GPT-4o, Claude Sonnet 3.7, Gemini 2.0 Flash
- **Tier 3 (Budget)**: Qwen QwQ-32B, DeepSeek V3, Mixtral 8x7B

#### Process
1. Each model generates all 16 perspectives
2. Track: completion rate, word count, citation density, generation time
3. Save outputs in structured format

#### Outputs
- 16 perspectives × 10 models = **160 documents**
- Metadata: model, timestamp, tokens, cost, completion status
- Compiled markdown and PDF per model

---

### Phase 2: Quality Scoring (Weeks 5-6)

**Objective**: Evaluate quality across multiple dimensions

#### Scoring Rubric (1-7 Likert Scale)

**Scientific Rigor**
- Accepts AR7 assessment as baseline (mandatory)
- Citation quality and density
- Factual accuracy
- Evidence-based reasoning

**Internal Consistency**
- Coherent worldview throughout
- Logical flow from principles to implementation
- No contradictions within perspective

**Feasibility Assessment**
- Realistic implementation pathways
- Addresses practical barriers honestly
- Resource requirements specified

**Strategic Depth**
- Sophisticated analysis of challenges
- Nuanced understanding of trade-offs
- Concrete mechanisms, not platitudes

**Stylistic Quality**
- Appropriate tone for perspective
- Engaging and readable
- Proper structure and organization

**Completeness**
- All framework elements addressed
- ~5000 word target met
- Comprehensive reference list

#### Scoring Process
- Human evaluation of random sample (20%)
- AI-assisted scoring using GPT-5/Claude for remainder
- Inter-rater reliability checks
- Statistical analysis of results

---

### Phase 3: Comparative Analysis (Weeks 7-8)

**Objective**: Compare perspectives across key dimensions

#### Comparison Dimensions

**1. Governance Models**
- Centralized ↔ Decentralized
- Hierarchical ↔ Horizontal
- State-led ↔ Community-led
- Technocratic ↔ Democratic

**2. Economic Systems**
- Growth-based ↔ Degrowth
- Capitalist ↔ Post-capitalist
- Market ↔ Commons/Planning
- Private ↔ Public ownership

**3. Technology Approach**
- High-tech ↔ Appropriate tech
- Large-scale ↔ Distributed
- Geoengineering ↔ Nature-based
- Innovation-focused ↔ Sufficiency-focused

**4. Justice Frameworks**
- Utilitarian ↔ Rights-based
- Individual ↔ Collective
- Procedural ↔ Distributive
- Retributive ↔ Restorative

**5. Timeline & Urgency**
- Immediate action ↔ Phased transition
- Revolutionary ↔ Reformist
- Emergency powers ↔ Deliberative process

**6. Geographic Scale**
- Global ↔ Local
- Universal ↔ Context-specific
- Coordinated ↔ Autonomous

#### Analysis Methods
- Multidimensional scaling (MDS) plots
- Cluster analysis of similar perspectives
- Network analysis of overlapping strategies
- Quantitative text analysis for themes

---

### Phase 4: Tournament Evaluation (Weeks 9-12)

**Objective**: Head-to-head comparisons across criteria

#### Tournament Brackets

**Bracket A: Governance Showdown**
- State-led (Chinese, AI Technocracy, Post-WWIII) vs.
- Community-led (Indigenous, Anarchist, Bioregional, Solarpunk)

**Bracket B: Economic Systems**
- Market-based (Amazon, Billionaire Cabal) vs.
- Commons-based (Degrowth, Anarchist, Indigenous)

**Bracket C: Technology Philosophy**
- High-tech (SF Triumphalism, Geoengineering, AI) vs.
- Appropriate-tech (Solarpunk, Bioregional, Indigenous)

**Bracket D: Justice Frameworks**
- Equity-focused (SIDS, Youth, Indigenous, Islamic) vs.
- Efficiency-focused (Amazon, Chinese, AI, Geoengineering)

#### Evaluation Criteria (per bracket)

**Effectiveness Potential** (1-10)
- Likely to achieve adaptation goals?
- Addresses scale and urgency?
- Overcomes collective action problems?

**Equity & Justice** (1-10)
- Protects vulnerable populations?
- Addresses historical responsibility?
- Fair distribution of costs/benefits?

**Feasibility** (1-10)
- Political economy realistic?
- Implementation pathway clear?
- Can overcome resistance?

**Resilience** (1-10)
- Robust to shocks and surprises?
- Adaptive and learning-oriented?
- Avoids catastrophic failure modes?

**Desirability** (1-10)
- Creates world worth living in?
- Protects human dignity and rights?
- Maintains democratic values?

#### Judging Panel
- Climate scientists (assess scientific grounding)
- Policy experts (assess feasibility)
- Ethicists (assess justice dimensions)
- Systems thinkers (assess complexity handling)
- Public survey (assess public acceptability)

---

### Phase 5: Synthesis & Insights (Weeks 13-14)

**Objective**: Extract insights across all perspectives

#### Key Questions

**1. What do ALL perspectives agree on?**
- Core truths that transcend ideology
- Unavoidable adaptation necessities
- Universal implementation challenges

**2. What are irreconcilable differences?**
- Fundamental value conflicts
- Trade-offs with no win-win solutions
- Incommensurable worldviews

**3. What hybrid approaches emerge?**
- Best elements from multiple perspectives
- Complementary strategies at different scales
- Sequencing different approaches

**4. What blind spots appear across all perspectives?**
- Challenges no perspective adequately addresses
- Unexamined assumptions
- Missing considerations

**5. Which perspectives perform best overall?**
- By quality scores
- By tournament results
- By criteria weighting

#### Synthesis Outputs

**Academic Paper**
- "16 Paths to Climate Adaptation: A Comparative Analysis"
- Published in Nature Climate Change or similar

**Policy Brief**
- "Lessons from Alternative Climate Strategies"
- For policymakers and practitioners

**Public Report**
- Accessible summary with visualizations
- Interactive website with full documents

**Meta-Analysis**
- What this exercise reveals about climate discourse
- Role of worldviews in climate response
- AI capabilities for perspective-taking

## Ethical Considerations

### Thought Experiments
- **Evil Billionaire Cabal**: Clearly marked as thought experiment exploring elite capture
- **Post-WWIII**: Hypothetical scenario for understanding compound crises
- Purpose: Critical examination, not endorsement

### Perspective Authenticity
- Engage experts from each tradition for review
- Avoid caricature or strawman versions
- Acknowledge AI limitations in representing lived experience
- Invite commentary from represented communities

### Fairness & Balance
- No perspective pre-judged as "correct"
- Evaluation criteria applied consistently
- Acknowledge author's positionality and biases
- Present findings, not prescriptions

### Public Communication
- Clear framing: AI-generated explorations, not authoritative
- Emphasize learning value over "winner" determination
- Avoid sensationalism of extreme perspectives
- Promote constructive dialogue
